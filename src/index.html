About this model

Eight nitrogen pools share one conserved total: starting nitrogen + inputs = nitrogen now, including what drained away. Every moving particle comes from a real transfer; glowing dots sitting in the soil show how big each pool is.

One second at 1× is half an illustrative day. Rates depend on available nitrogen, microbe activity, moisture and oxygen. Fertilizer is 60% NH₄⁺ and 40% NO₃⁻; dead leaves add 8 units; lightning moves N₂ into nitrate gradually.

Simplified on purpose: no temperature, pH, immobilisation, ammonia loss or separate denitrification gases. Plants here take up nitrate (real plants can also use ammonium). Particles are visual cues, not one unit each. Microbe pictures are illustrations.
