DESIGNED FOR EXPLORATION

One connected system.

Moving particles are proportional visual cues emitted only by real transfers in this model. The eight nitrogen reservoirs share a conserved total: initial nitrogen + recorded external inputs = current nitrogen, including cumulative drainage loss.

The simulation uses fixed 0.05-day steps. One second at 1× advances half an illustrative day. Speed changes how quickly time advances, not the conversion rules. Rates respond to available nitrogen, microbial activity, soil moisture and oxygen availability.

Fertilizer adds 60% NH₄⁺ and 40% NO₃⁻. Dead leaves add 8 N units. Lightning transfers N₂ from the atmosphere into nitrate gradually. Growth encourages uptake; it never creates nitrogen.

This finite, closed educational landscape simplifies a much larger real ecosystem. It omits temperature, pH, immobilization, ammonia volatilization, microbial nitrogen biomass and separate denitrification gases. Plant uptake follows the supplied textbook’s nitrate pathway; real plants can also absorb ammonium. Biological fixation produces ammonia/ammonium first, with nitrate formed through nitrification. Particle counts are proportional cues; each particle is not one N unit.
